The 5G-MAG Steering Group has approved phine.tech as a new member of the association.

{/* truncate */}

Headquartered in Vienna, Austria, phine.tech builds tools that make 5G more approachable for industrial and developer use cases, aiming to close the gap between 5G vendors and the industries that need them. Its flagship product, the Virtual 5G Lab (V5GL), replaces lengthy hardware setup with a cloud-based, developer-friendly environment that's ready in seconds — supporting use cases from robotics and manufacturing to research, with the same approach already extending toward 6G.
