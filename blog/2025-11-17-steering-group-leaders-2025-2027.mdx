The 5G-MAG General Assembly has elected its new Steering Group to lead the association's activities over the next two years.

{/* truncate */}

The Steering Group sets the strategic direction of 5G-MAG and oversees the association's activities across standards, open-source development and industry outreach. The full composition of the new Steering Group, along with how it fits into 5G-MAG's wider governance, is available on the [Structure](/structure) page.

5G-MAG is a not-for-profit association fostering collaboration to shape the future of connected media experiences, bridging standards and open-source implementation so that services, applications and networks stay interoperable and future-proof.
