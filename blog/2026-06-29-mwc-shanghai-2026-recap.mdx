At MWC Shanghai (24–26 June, SNIEC), 5G-MAG showcased the live demonstration "Towards Media-Aware Intelligent Networks — Smarter Video Streaming for 6G" at Qualcomm's booth.

{/* truncate */}

The demo was an open-source implementation of the [3GPP 5G Media Streaming](/reference-tools/5gms) specifications, incorporating Common Media Client Data (CMCD) from CTA WAVE, powered by the 5G-MAG Reference Tools with contributions from Qualcomm, BBC, Fraunhofer FOKUS and Dolby.

A massive shout-out to Shilin Ding for driving the demo forward.
