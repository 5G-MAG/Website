On 21 October 2026, Frédéric Gabin, 5G-MAG Chair, took part in the 3GPP Project Coordination Group meeting (PCG#57) and presented the latest news from 5G-MAG. The contribution, PCG57_25, is listed on the [liaison statements page](/ls).

{/* truncate */}

The news slides cover five areas:

- **6G and 6G Media.** The 6G AI Traffic Characterization testbed, avatar calls on phones and a reference SWAP server, with the 3GPP specifications they follow.
- **Advanced Media Delivery.** CMCD and QoE metrics reporting in 5G Media Streaming, and a CMMF encoder.
- **5G MBS and 5G Broadcast.** Rel-18 MBS functions, a full gNB stack for MBS broadcast, and the 5G Broadcast work that follows Rel-19.
- **5G Broadcast PlugFest.** Four vendors tested interoperability at Fraunhofer FOKUS in Berlin on 9 to 11 June 2026. Read the [PlugFest report](/news/plugfest-berlin-2026).
- **The 2026 feedback loop.** Issues raised on 3GPP specifications through GitHub, and the change requests that came back from 3GPP groups. See [issue tracking](/standards/3gpp-issue-tracking).

A second deck gives an overview of 5G-MAG's scope, projects and ways to join. Both decks are in the PCG57_25 download on the [liaison statements page](/ls).
