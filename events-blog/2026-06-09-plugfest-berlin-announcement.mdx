5G-MAG organizes its first 5G Broadcast PlugFest, bringing infrastructure vendors, technology providers, developers and operators together to test interoperability.

**When:** 9–11 June · **Where:** Fraunhofer FOKUS, Berlin

Registration is open for 5G-MAG's first 5G Broadcast PlugFest, three days of hands-on, multi-vendor interoperability testing bringing together equipment vendors, receiver and middleware developers, and operators. Participants will pair core networks and transmitters from different suppliers and work through a shared test plan spanning the full delivery chain — from core-to-transmitter interfaces to application-layer services.

Interested teams can register to take part, and the community is invited to follow along and get involved via GitHub and Slack in the run-up to the event.
