**When:** 2–5 March · **Where:** Barcelona — Stand 5C30, Swiss Pavilion

5G-MAG returns to Mobile World Congress Barcelona with a stand in the Swiss Pavilion, where visitors can see live demonstrations of the association's open-source Reference Tools in action across media streaming, broadcast and immersive media use cases.

Jordi J. Gimenez, 5G-MAG's Head of Technology, will be on-site throughout the show and available to talk through the Reference Tools roadmap and how to get involved as a contributor. 5G-MAG will also host networking drinks at the stand on the opening day of the show — a chance to meet the team and the wider developer community in person.

Full programme details, including specific demo slots, will be published closer to the event.
