Organized by Fraunhofer FOKUS, the 2026 edition of the Media Web Symposium explored AI-driven creativity, immersive experiences and sustainable practices in media technology. 5G-MAG was on-site with demos of its open-source Reference Tools and contributed to presentations covering generative workflows, XR storytelling, and green streaming optimization.
