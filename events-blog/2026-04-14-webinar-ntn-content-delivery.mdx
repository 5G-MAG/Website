5G-MAG and the European Space Agency's NTN Forum co-host a webinar on non-terrestrial networks and media delivery, from specification to working software.

**When:** 14 April @ 13:00–15:30 CEST · **Where:** Online (MS Teams)

Speakers from industry and academia cover how non-terrestrial networks (NTN) intersect with content delivery in practice, including:

- Open-source implementations of 5G multicast/broadcast services over NTN
- Direct-to-device satellite technology
- Edge orchestration frameworks for satellite-served content delivery

The session is aimed at anyone working at the intersection of satellite connectivity and media delivery who wants a grounded, implementation-level view rather than a purely theoretical one.
