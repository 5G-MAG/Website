5G-MAG brings a live demo of its 5G Multicast Broadcast Services (MBS) reference tools, including Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) content delivery, to the NTN Forum's Colloquium & General Assembly.

**When:** 31 August – 2 September · **Where:** ESA/ESTEC, Keplerlaan 1, Noordwijk, Netherlands

Organized by ESA's Space for 5G/6G & Sustainable Connectivity programme, the NTN Forum brings together stakeholders from across the satellite and terrestrial telecommunications industries to discuss the deployment and operation of satellite-enabled services based on 3GPP standards.

5G-MAG will be on-site with an end-to-end demonstration of its 3GPP Release 18 [5G Multicast Broadcast Services (MBS)](/reference-tools/5g-mbs) reference tools for content delivery over 5G, including delivery over Non-Terrestrial Networks. The demo is brought by BBC and Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV), contributors to the MBS reference tools project.
