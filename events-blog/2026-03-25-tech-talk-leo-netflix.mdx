**When:** 25 March @ 15:00–16:00 CET · **Where:** Online (Zoom)

Dr. Renata Teixeira from Netflix presents an analysis of Netflix streaming performance over Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite networks, examining quality of experience metrics drawn from real Netflix data spanning millions of households across 147 countries.

The talk characterizes how LEO networks affect video streaming performance in practice — including throughput fluctuations and the impact of packet loss — giving the 5G-MAG community a data-backed view of what LEO connectivity means for media delivery today.
