**When:** 27 February @ 13:00 CET · **Where:** Online (Zoom) — 5G-MAG's [Dev Public Call](/public-call)

5G-MAG is launching a new open-source project enabling testing and characterizing how Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications interact with next-generation networks. The initiative includes:

- A scenario engine supporting 11 different AI interaction types
- A network emulator simulating 3GPP network conditions
- Metric collection tracking over 60 KPIs relevant to AI performance on 6G systems

This online session on the monthly Dev Public Call invites industry participants and developers to explore this new reference tool as it opens up for testing and contributions.
