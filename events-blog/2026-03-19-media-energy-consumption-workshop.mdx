**When:** 19 March @ 15:00–17:00 CET · **Where:** Online (Zoom)

This joint workshop explored how to measure and optimize energy consumption in media streaming, with a particular focus on 5G and 6G systems. Experts from operators, standards bodies, industry and academia discussed:

- Ongoing 3GPP work on energy measurement and exposure
- The challenges of measuring energy consumption in cloud environments
- Encoding efficiency and its impact on end-to-end energy use
- Emerging IETF work relevant to energy-aware media delivery
- Reference tools and prototypes for building energy-aware solutions

The workshop was co-organized by 5G-MAG together with 3GPP SA4 and Greening of Streaming, bringing together the standards and open-source sides of the conversation in one session.
