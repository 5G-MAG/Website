:::tip[At a glance]

- **What it is:** the testbed SA4 uses to measure the traffic of AI media services for its 6G media study, TR 26.870 (FS_6G_MED, Release 20).
- **How:** AI service scenarios run over emulated network conditions; the traffic is captured and turned into metrics.
- **Status:** TR 26.870 is a draft (V0.6.1, September 2026); its results are initial.

:::

## What it does

Traffic characteristics of media services are relevant to the design of 6G radio and service architectures. AI media services are new, so the study measures them with this testbed, and records the results as the basis for its statements on AI media services.

## What it has found

| Question | Initial answer in TR 26.870 |
| --- | --- |
| Is AI traffic uplink heavy? | For some scenarios, such as video sent for inference and the agents evaluated. Others are heavily downlink. |
| Bursts and delay sensitivity? | Bursty, periodic or not, delay sensitive or tolerant depending on the application. |
| Does round-trip delay matter? | Yes, many AI applications are interactive; connection setup adds to it. |
| Does traffic vary within one application? | Yes, on every dimension measured. |
| Tokenized traffic? | Seen on the uplink where the client tokenizes media, and on the downlink when output is streamed. |

See [Results](./results) for the figures behind each answer.
