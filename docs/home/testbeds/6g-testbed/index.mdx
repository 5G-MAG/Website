:::tip[At a glance]

- **What it is:** the testbed SA4 uses to measure the traffic of AI media services for its 6G media study, TR 26.870 (FS_6G_MED, Release 20).
- **How:** AI service scenarios run over emulated network conditions; the traffic is captured and turned into metrics.
- **Where in the TR:** annex B describes the testbed, annex C the method and results, annex D the scenarios, network profiles, metrics and real-time video results, and annex E the network emulator (a temporary annex). See [How the testbed maps to the annexes](/testbeds/6g-testbed/scope#how-the-testbed-maps-to-the-annexes).
- **Status:** TR 26.870 is a draft (V0.6.1, September 2026); its results are initial.

:::

## What it does

The 6G media study records the traffic characteristics of AI media services from this testbed's measurements, as the basis for its statements on AI media services. The study obtains the characteristics with the framework of its annexes B, C and D (clause 6.3.3.1); the results are the record of the AI traffic characteristics it collects. What the study covers, and where AI traffic fits in it: [Technical Analysis](/tech/6g/overview).
