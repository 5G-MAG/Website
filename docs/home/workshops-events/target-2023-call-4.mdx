## Agenda — 5G Media Use Cases and Applications

After receiving a total of 23 use cases and identifying the main functions required to implement them, this call brought the developers' team together to review and discuss them directly.

Participants who had already registered for a previous call in the series did not need to register again, as the same [Zoom registration link](https://us06web.zoom.us/meeting/register/tZcldO2orD4vHtWrYnWKiCbRZ3leYhiecz3Q) was reused across all four calls.

## Part of the Target 2023 series

This was Call 4 of 4 in the Target 2023 process. Full context on the process is available on the [Target 2023 page](http://www.5g-mag.com/target2023).
