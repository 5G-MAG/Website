The event combines presentations and a round table discussion, bringing together industry experts to share insights and discuss the current status, opportunities and challenges in the field.

### At 10:00 — The Intro: Welcome

Introduction by Samina Husain (IEEE BTS).

### At 10:00 — Session 1, Media Delivery: Scaling Media Services

Next steps in media delivery. Session Chair: Daniel Silhavy (Fraunhofer FOKUS).

**Confirmed speakers:** Valentijn Siebrands (Streaming Solution Manager, Unified Streaming) — media technology in the rise of streaming; Will Law (Chief Architect - Cloud Technology Group, Akamai) — streaming, scalability, MoQ and more.

### At 10:45 — Session 2, New Technology: AI, Networks &amp; Media

Towards autonomous workflows? Session Chair: Frédéric Gabin (Dolby, 5G-MAG Chair).

**Confirmed speakers:** Michael Stattmann (CEO, Castlabs) — content provenance, authenticity and C2PA; Saba Ahsan (Principal Research Lead, Nokia) — future networks in the age of 6G and AI; Thierry Fautier (Lead Business Strategist, The Media League).

### At 11:30 — Session Break: Paper and Demo Pitches at IBC

Introduced by Jordi J. Gimenez (5G-MAG). Speakers: shortly confirmed.

### At 11:45 — Session 3, Video Codecs: Next-G Video Codecs

Video codecs towards 2030. Session Chair: Thomas Stockhammer (Qualcomm).

**Confirmed speakers:** Julien Lemotheux (Senior Standardization Manager, Orange), Yan Ye (Distinguished Scientist, Alibaba Group U.S) and Mickaël Raulet (CTO, ATEME) — insights on next-generation video codecs.

### At 12:30 — Session 4, Panel Discussion: Fresh Updates

Panel to be announced soon. Session Chair: TBC. Speakers: shortly confirmed.

A range of topics — the transition to online-only delivery, streaming tech, non-terrestrial networks, immersive media, XR and immersive story-telling — were covered, together with a panel discussion on 5G Broadcast with insights from stakeholders with concrete launch plans.

### At 10:00 — Insights from the industry, with Q&amp;A

Introduction by Maurizio Murroni (IEEE BTS). Session Chair: Emily Dubs (DVB).

**The Future of Media Distribution: Transiting to online and streaming** — with Tim Pullen (BBC), Will Law (Akamai Technologies), Joel Grotz (SES) and Glenn Deen (Comcast).

**The Future of Media Experiences: XR for Sports and Entertainment, and 6G** — with Eddie Drake (The Walt Disney Studios), José Somolinos (Accedo), Frédéric Plourde (Collabora &amp; The Khronos Group), Thomas Stockhammer (Qualcomm) and Gilles Teniou (3GPP SA4 Chair).

### At 12:00 — Short break with 5G-MAG demos &amp; paper pitches

### At 12:15 — Panel discussion: 5G Broadcast, ready for launch?

With Mohamed Aziz Taga (Rohde &amp; Schwarz), Markus Schneider (Media Broadcast), Johann Mika (Big Blue Marble) and Aytac Biber (Qualcomm).

At the end of the event, attendees joined the 5G-MAG Meet, Greet (&amp; Eat) at Stand 10.D21.
