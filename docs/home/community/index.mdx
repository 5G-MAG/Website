5G-MAG doesn't write specifications — it turns them into working, open-source software, built by the same industry that writes the standards. That code is developed in the open, by engineers from broadcasters, vendors, and research labs working side by side, and it feeds real implementation experience straight back into 3GPP and MPEG. If you build media or connectivity software, this is where you get a running start on the next generation of it, and a say in how it's specified.

## Becoming a Contributor

This is the legal and organisational side of contributing — joining as a contributor and signing the paperwork that lets your code be merged.

1. Sign an Individual or Corporate Contributor License Agreement (CLA).
2. Anybody can become a contributor — no need to be a 5G-MAG member to contribute code.
3. 5G-MAG members get priority support from the 5G-MAG Project Office, and additional development resources may be provided — members define priorities and where to dedicate resources.
4. Membership of 5G-MAG remains open to the industry, to boost and scale your software project by joining forces.

In order to clarify the intellectual property license granted with contributions from any person or entity willing to contribute under the 5G-MAG Public License, a **Contributor License Agreement (CLA)** is required to be on file signed by each Contributor, indicating agreement to the license terms. We do this for your protection as a contributor as well as the protection of 5G-MAG and its users; and it does not change your rights to use your own contributions for any other purpose.

If you wish to contribute code to the projects please download the CLA or CCLA and send it signed to Eva Markvoort [markvoort@5g-mag.com](mailto:markvoort@5g-mag.com).

- Download the [**5G-MAG Individual Contributor License Agreement (CLA)**](pathname:///Getting-Started/OFFICIAL_5G-MAG_Contributor_License_Agreement.pdf)
- Download the [**5G-MAG Corporate Contributor License Agreement (CCLA)**](pathname:///Getting-Started/OFFICIAL_5G-MAG_Corporate_Contributor_License_Agreement.pdf)

## Developer Tools

Assets for 5G-MAG members that support the transition of standards into deployments: tools, rules and working records for going from a specification to a working implementation. The tools are the 3GPP Explorer, the AI Development Guidelines, the Spec API Validator and the exploration tools. [See the Developer Tools](/developer-tools).
