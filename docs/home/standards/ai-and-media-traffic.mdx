## Overview

AI and Media Traffic has two testbeds, and each has its own Standards page.

## Specifications in scope

- [Standards: AI/ML Evaluation Framework](/standards/ai-ml): TR 26.927 and TR 26.847, on AI and ML in 5G media services, with the specifications they relate to.
- [Standards: Towards 6G Media](/standards/6g): TR 26.870, the study on media aspects for 6G, which the 6G AI Traffic Characterization Testbed measures for.
