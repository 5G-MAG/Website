## Overview

5G-MAG tracks the early standardisation of media aspects for 6G systems. 6G work in 3GPP is currently at the study stage, so the focus here is on the relevant studies rather than finished specifications. What the SA4 media study covers is on the [Technical Analysis](/tech/6g/overview) page; the study/work-item history behind these reports is on [Standards Evolution](/standards/6g/evolution). 5G-MAG's inputs to the 3GPP 6G workshops are on the [Requirements](/standards/requirements) page.

## Specifications by release

6G is being defined in two parallel processes: ITU-R (IMT-2030) sets the global framework, and 3GPP writes the specifications.

- **ITU-R framework**: [Recommendation ITU-R M.2160-0](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/m/R-REC-M.2160-0-202311-I!!PDF-E.pdf), "Framework and overall objectives of the future development of IMT for 2030 and beyond" (published November 2023).
- **Release 20**: the SA4 media study, TR 26.870, is a Release 20 study. It aims to meet the 6G system requirements developed in TR 22.870 and captured by TS 22.270, in alignment with the SA2 architecture study, TR 23.801-01. Its conclusions will form the basis for further studies and normative work.

Readers should treat all study-stage content as provisional.
