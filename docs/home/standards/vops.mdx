## Overview

3GPP TS 26.265 defines service-independent video operation points and capabilities, which 3GPP
service specifications and third-party services may reference. Its conformance annex provides a
conformance validator for testing bitstreams against those operation points, which is what the
[VOPS Conformance Validator](/testbeds/vops) testbed holds.
