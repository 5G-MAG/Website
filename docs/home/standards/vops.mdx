## Overview

TS 26.265 V19.2.0, Introduction: "The present document defines service-independent video operation points and capabilities." It adds: "These may be referenced in 3GPP service specifications or in third-party services."

TS 26.265 V19.2.0, clause 4.2: "The combination of video coding format and a specific representation format is referred to as Operation Point." The [VOPS Conformance Validator](/testbeds/vops) testbed holds the conformance validator of annex B.2.3 and is part of the Video Operation Points project.

## What the specification defines

- **Capabilities.** TS 26.265 V19.2.0, clause 4.3 defines a video decoding capability and a video encoding capability. It states: "While not explicitly stated in the capabilities, it is a requirement for decoders and receivers to process the data in real-time."
- **Codecs.** Clause 5.2.1 lists AVC/H.264 Progressive High Profile, HEVC/H.265 Main and Main-10 Profile Main Tier, and HEVC/H.265 Multiview Main 10 and Multiview Extended 10 Main Tier.
- **Requirement level.** Clause 5.1, NOTE: "These clause does not specify whether these capabilities are required, recommended or suggested to be supported." Service specifications or external specifications decide that.
- **Operation points.** Clause 6.1: "For each Video Operation Point, requirements for the Bitstream and for the Receiver are defined." Table 6.1-1 lists seven: 3GPP-AVC-HD, 3GPP-HEVC-HD, 3GPP-HEVC-HD-HDR, 3GPP-HEVC-UHD-HDR, 3GPP-HEVC-Stereo, 3GPP-MV-HEVC-Main-Stereo and 3GPP-MV-HEVC-Ext-Stereo.
- **System integration.** Clauses 7.2.2 (AVC) and 7.2.3 (HEVC) state: "The definitions for AVC are for further study." and "The definitions for HEVC are for further study."

## Conformance annex

TS 26.265 V19.2.0, annex B.1 lists five conformance functions: annotated video test signals, reference encoders with configurations, a conformance validator, conforming decoders, and conformance bitstreams. Annex B.3: "the conformance validator may be used to test bitstreams claiming to be conformant to an operation point indeed are conforming, or if not, which test assertions are not fulfilled."

Clause B.2.3: "The detailed semantics and the format of the report is for further study."

## Versions

5G-MAG's testbed is built against V19.2.0 (2026-03), which this page quotes. V19.3.0 also exists. Checked in V19.3.0, clause 5.3.3 is renamed "Dual-layer HEVC Decoding Capabilities" and now defines the two-layer cases through output layer sets, with a new table 5.3.3-1 of supported profile combinations. The baseline remains V19.2.0 until the validator is rebuilt against V19.3.0.

## Open points in the specification

- Annex A.2.3: "This work is for further study." (mapping of operation points to an encoder API).
- Operation point names are not used consistently. Table 6.1-1 uses "3GPP-HEVC-HD-HDR" and "3GPP-HEVC-UHD-HDR"; table A.2.2-1 lists "3GPP-HEVC-3DTV" and "3GPP-MV-HEVC-3DTV", names that do not appear in table 6.1-1. This is recorded in 5G-MAG's findings register and has not been raised in SA4.
