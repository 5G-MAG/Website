## Overview

5G-MAG tracks the early standardisation of media aspects for 6G systems. 6G work in 3GPP is currently at the study stage (technical reports on use cases and service requirements), so the focus here is on the relevant 3GPP studies rather than finished specifications. For the technical analysis of the IMT-2030 usage scenarios and the 5G-MAG 6G Testbed, see the Tech page linked below. For acronyms used here, see the [Glossary](/tech/glossary).

## Specifications by release

6G is being defined in two parallel processes: ITU-R (IMT-2030) sets the global framework, and 3GPP writes the specifications.

- **ITU-R framework**: [Recommendation ITU-R M.2160-0](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/m/R-REC-M.2160-0-202311-I!!PDF-E.pdf), "Framework and overall objectives of the future development of IMT for 2030 and beyond" (published November 2023). See the [Tech page](/tech/6g) for the usage-scenario detail.
- **Release 20** (current): study stage. SA1 has produced the Stage 1 requirements study (TR 22.870); the architecture and media groups (SA2, SA4) are running exploratory studies (TR 26.870) rather than normative specifications. No normative 6G specifications exist yet.
- **Release 21**: normative Stage 1 phase for 6G starts.
- Initial 6G specifications are expected around 2028.

Readers should treat all study-stage content as provisional.

## Key 3GPP Specifications

- **[TR 22.870](https://www.3gpp.org/dynareport/22870.htm) - Study on 6G Use Cases and Service Requirements** (SA1, Stage 1 requirements track). Clusters use cases around the IMT-2030 usage scenarios and derives candidate system requirements.
- **[TR 26.870](https://www.3gpp.org/dynareport/26870.htm) - Study on Media Aspects for 6G System** (SA4, media track, most relevant to 5G-MAG). Media services, formats, traffic characteristics and delivery for a 6G system, building on the SA4 5G media work (5G Media Streaming and the Data Collection and Reporting framework).

### Related AI/ML studies that feed 6G

These 5G Advanced studies are relevant to 6G media work and are covered in more detail on the [AI & ML](/standards/ai-ml) page:

- [TR 26.847](https://www.3gpp.org/dynareport/26847.htm) (SA4): Evaluation of AI and ML in 5G media services (Release 19, completed).
- [TR 22.874](https://www.3gpp.org/dynareport/22874.htm) (SA1): Study on traffic characteristics and performance requirements for AI/ML model transfer in 5GS.

## 5G-MAG tracking and contribution focus

5G-MAG follows the studies above and provides media-specific input to the 6G process. Publicly available inputs to 3GPP 6G workshops are listed on the [technical view of this topic](/tech/6g). The emphasis is on media delivery at extreme data rates, AI-assisted and AI-native management of media traffic, immersive and haptic experiences, and the evolution of broadcast and multicast towards 6G.
