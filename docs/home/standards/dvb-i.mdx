<h1>DVB-I Services over 5G</h1>
</div>
</div>

5G-MAG monitors and contributes to work on DVB-I service delivery over 5G systems. DVB-I (Digital Video Broadcasting - Internet) defines mechanisms for internet-connected devices to discover and access sets of linear TV services delivered over broadband or broadcast networks, together with the associated programme metadata. For DVB-I service discovery and its deployment over 5G, see the [Technology page](/tech/dvb-i).

The DVB / 5G-MAG Joint Task Force documented DVB-I deployment over 5G in ETSI TR 103 972.

5G-MAG follows the DVB / 5G-MAG Joint Task Force output and the downstream work in 3GPP and ETSI. The practical focus areas are:

- Tracking the recommended specification changes from TR 103 972 as they are taken up in DVB, 3GPP SA4, and ETSI JTC Broadcast.
- Interoperability between DVB-I discovery and 3GPP service announcement on the broadcast path.

:::tip[At a glance]

- **Service discovery:** DVB A177, published by ETSI as TS 103 770. V1.2.1 (September 2024) is the latest published version; A177r8 is a draft of V1.3.1.
- **Deployment over 5G:** ETSI TR 103 972 V1.1.1 (July 2023), from the DVB / 5G-MAG Joint Task Force. It is the only version.
- **Delivery over 5G:** unicast over 5G Media Streaming, broadcast over LTE-based 5G Broadcast (ETSI TS 103 720), and hybrid combinations.

:::

## Release by release

DVB-I over 5G spans three standards bodies, so there is no single release line. The dependencies track as follows:

- **DVB**: A177 (published in successive revisions as ETSI TS 103 770; the latest published ETSI version is V1.2.1, and A177r8 is a draft of V1.3.1) is the stable service-discovery base; ETSI TR 103 972 V1.1.1 (2023) is a technical report rather than a normative specification, so it records guidance and gaps rather than mandating behaviour. The commercial input came from DVB C100 (2021).
- **3GPP SA4**: the 5GMS architecture (TS 26.501) was introduced in Release 16 and has been extended in later releases; the MBS user-service work (TS 26.502) and the underlying system architecture (TS 23.247) were introduced in Release 17 and continue to evolve. The Release 18 5G Media Streaming Protocols Phase 2 work item added support for 5GMS over MBS and hybrid 5GMS services (TR 21.918 clause 17.9), and Release 19 Advanced Media Delivery added in-session unicast repair for MBS object distribution and deployment guidelines for MBS User Services over eMBMS (TR 21.919 clause 11.6).
- **ETSI JTC Broadcast**: the LTE-based 5G Broadcast profile (TS 103 720) has been aligned to successive 3GPP releases, with a work item updating it toward Release 18 (v1.3.1).

Because TR 103 972 is a technical report, its main output is a set of recommended changes to specifications owned by DVB, 3GPP and ETSI; the follow-up happens in those bodies rather than in a single DVB-I-over-5G specification.

## Delivery over 5G

ETSI TR 103 972 maps the commercial requirements for DVB-I over 5G (captured in DVB BlueBook C100, the DVB commercial-requirements document) into a reference architecture and deployment guidance covering three service scenarios:

<SpecTable
  columns={["Mode", "Bearer", "Typical use"]}
  rows={[
    ["Unicast", "5G Media Streaming (5GMS)", "On-demand and linear services delivered per device over broadband 5G"],
    ["Broadcast", "LTE-based 5G Broadcast", "One-way, wide-area linear TV and radio to many devices"],
    ["Concurrent / hybrid", "5G Broadcast and ordinary unicast", "The same service over both bearers, or hybrid DVB-I services combining them"],
  ]}
/>

The report also records gaps identified in existing specifications under the control of DVB, 3GPP and ETSI. For the reference architecture and scenario-by-scenario detail, see [DVB-I Services over 5G: Technology](/tech/dvb-i).
