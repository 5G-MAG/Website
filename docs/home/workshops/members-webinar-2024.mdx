## About the Webinar

Join us for a 1-hour webinar on 5G-MAG, followed by a 30-minute Q&A session. This webinar is open to both new and existing members.

It's a great opportunity to:

- Gain insights into all our current work at 5G-MAG
- Ask any questions you may have
- Learn about the upcoming 5G-MAG Sessions at IBC

:::note Members only
This webinar was organized for 5G-MAG members. The recording and joining details are available exclusively through the [5G-MAG Members' Area](https://member.5g-mag.com/wg/GA/calendar/event/259/2024-06-27), and are not publicly accessible.
:::
