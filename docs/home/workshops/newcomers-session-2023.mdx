A brief, to-the-point orientation for people who had recently joined 5G-MAG, or who worked at a member organization and wanted insight into the association's work.

## What this session covered

During this 1-hour session, the agenda went through:

- Current work items and where they are discussed
- Main outcomes and where to find them
- The workgroups and how to participate
- How to bring your ideas (if they are not covered in existing work)
- Working methods
- Open communities (Google Group, Slack and GitHub)
- How to use the Members' platform

The recording is available to members via the [Members' platform](https://member.5g-mag.com/wg/GA/wiki/members-only-events).

If you work at any of the companies listed on the [membership page](https://www.5g-mag.com/membership), you are welcome to join future 5G-MAG Newcomers' Sessions.
