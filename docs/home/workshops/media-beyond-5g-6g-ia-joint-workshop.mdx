As part of 5G-MAG's engagement with 6G-IA (the 6G Smart Networks and Services Industry Association, following the MoU signed between 6G-IA and 5G-MAG), the two organizations agreed to organize this workshop for an exchange between 5G-MAG members and participants in research projects relevant to media and mobile connectivity features and solutions (5G, 5G Advanced and 6G).

The workshop was designed to be hands-on: each project gave a brief 12-minute presentation highlighting the technical problem it is trying to solve, followed by open discussion on requirements, features, technology readiness and prototyping.
