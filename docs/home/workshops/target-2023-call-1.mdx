## Agenda

**Introduction, Scope and Roadmap Planning** — David Vargas (BBC / 5G-MAG CD-T Chair)

**Rel-16 5G Media Streaming - Introduction** — Thorsten Lohmar (Ericsson) and Frédéric Gabin (Dolby, 3GPP SA4 Chair)

**Rel-16 5G Media Streaming - Dynamic network QoS policies** — Thorsten Lohmar (Ericsson) and Imed Bouazizi (Qualcomm)

**Rel-17 5G Media Streaming over eMBMS** — Thomas Stockhammer (Qualcomm) and Richard Bradbury (BBC)

**Rel-17 5G Multicast Broadcast Services** — Richard Bradbury (BBC) and Thorsten Lohmar (Ericsson)

**Rel-17 Event exposure framework** — Richard Bradbury (BBC), Chales Lo (Qualcomm) and Imed Bouazizi (Qualcomm)
