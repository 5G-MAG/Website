:::tip[At a glance]

- **What is supported:** transmission of a broadcast MBS session over NR non-terrestrial networks (NTN). The NTN additions were made in Release 19.
- **What stays the same:** a broadcast MBS session uses 5GC Shared MBS traffic delivery with PTM delivery only, and the UE receives it in RRC_IDLE, RRC_INACTIVE or RRC_CONNECTED.
- **What NTN adds:** an Intended Service Area (ISA), a geographical area signalled for MBS broadcast services. It can denote an area smaller than a cell.
- **Where the ISA travels:** from the NEF/MBSF, inside the MBS service area, through the MB-SMF and the AMF to NG-RAN, then to the UE in SIB27, with each session linked to its ISAs in the MCCH.
- **What the UE does with it:** inside an ISA it may start receiving the session and prioritise its frequency; on leaving the ISA it may stop. How the UE decides whether it is inside an ISA is left to the UE implementation.
- **Not supported:** location-dependent MBS services for broadcast over NR NTN.

:::

## Broadcast MBS sessions over NTN

Release 19 states that transmission of a Broadcast MBS Session is supported over NR non-terrestrial networks, and that TS 38.300 specifies the additional NG-RAN procedures for it (TS 23.247 clauses 4.1 and 6.21). Release 18 of TS 23.247 has no NTN text.

The broadcast MBS session itself is the one described in [MBS Broadcast - End-to-End Procedures](/tech/5g-mbs/analysis-mbs-broadcast-ran): the same service and content go to all UEs in a geographical area. For MBS broadcast, only the 5GC Shared MBS traffic delivery method with PTM delivery applies (TS 23.247 clause 4.1). The functions involved are those of the [MBS System architecture](/tech/5g-mbs/architecture-system).

What NTN adds is the Intended Service Area (ISA). An ISA is a geographical area that can be signalled for MBS broadcast services. It is broadcast in SIB27 and associated with MBS broadcast services by their ISA identifiers in the MCCH. It may also be included in the User Service Description (USD) within the target service area (TS 38.300 clause 16.14.12). In the 5G Core, the same information is called the NR NTN Intended Service Area: the "MBS Intended Service Area List" of TS 38.413 and TS 38.331 (TS 23.247 clause 3.1).

## The Intended Service Area in the 5G Core

For NR NTN, an MBS service area in the 5G Core may be denoted with an NR NTN Intended Service Area together with a TAI list and/or a cell ID list, for instance to denote an area smaller than a cell. NG-RAN uses the NR NTN Intended Service Area to further restrict the MBS service area denoted by the TAIs and/or cell IDs (TS 23.247 clause 6.21).

- **AF and NEF/MBSF.** Based on local configuration, the NEF may determine that an MBS session targets an NR NTN network, derive the NR NTN Intended Service Area from the geographical area information, and include it in the MBS service area it gives the MB-SMF at MBS session creation (TS 23.247 clause 6.21). TS 23.247 recommends that, based on service agreement, the AF uses a geographic area in an encoding compatible with TS 38.413 (clause 6.21, NOTE 1). The derivation is part of step 8, and its inclusion towards the MB-SMF part of step 11, of MBS Session Creation (clause 7.1.1.2).
- **Cell IDs.** For NR NTN, the cell IDs in the MBS service area can be mapped cell IDs (TS 23.247 clause 7.1.1.2, NOTE 5). The Cell Identity used for MBS broadcast service corresponds to a Mapped Cell ID (TS 38.300 clause 16.14.5).
- **MB-SMF and AMF.** The NR NTN Intended Service Area is transparently transmitted in the 5G Core: the MB-SMF selects the AMFs based on TAIs, and the AMF selects the NG-RAN nodes in the same manner (TS 23.247 clause 6.21, NOTE 2). At MBS Session Start for broadcast, the MB-SMF forwards the MBS service area including the NR NTN Intended Service Area to the selected AMFs (clause 7.3.1, step 2).
- **Not supported.** Location-dependent MBS services for broadcast over NR NTN are not supported in this release (TS 23.247 clause 6.21, NOTE 3).

## NGAP, from the AMF to NG-RAN

The AMF provides the MBS service area information towards the NG-RAN nodes as a cell ID list, a TAI list and/or an MBS intended service area list (TS 23.247 clause 7.3.1, step 3; TS 38.300 clause 16.14.5). In NGAP, the MBS Service Area Information IE carries an MBS Intended Service Area List, and each entry's Intended Service Area Coordinates IE includes the `areaCoordinates` of the `IntendedServiceAreaInfo` IE of TS 38.331 (TS 38.413 clauses 9.3.1.209 and 9.3.1.273).

If the MBS Service Area IE is in the BROADCAST SESSION SETUP REQUEST, the NG-RAN node takes it into account as specified in TS 23.247, and in TS 38.300 for NTN operation (TS 38.413 clause 8.17.1.2). On a BROADCAST SESSION MODIFICATION REQUEST, for NTN operation, the NG-RAN node updates the intended service area information, if supported (clause 8.17.2.2). This follows MBS Session Update for broadcast, where the MB-SMF and the AMF handle the NR NTN Intended Service Area as at session start (TS 23.247 clause 7.3.3, steps 2 and 3).

## System information: SIB19, SIB27 and the MCCH

| Block | What it carries | Clause |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SIB19 | Satellite assistance information for NTN access: NTN-specific parameters for the serving cell and, optionally, for neighbour cells | TS 38.331 cl. 6.3.1; TS 38.300 cl. 7.3.1 |
| SIB20 | The information required to acquire the MCCH/MTCH configuration for MBS broadcast | TS 38.331 cl. 6.3.1 |
| SIB21 | The mapping between the current and/or neighbouring carrier frequencies and MBS Frequency Selection Area Identities (FSAI) | TS 38.331 cl. 6.3.1 |
| SIB27 | The intended service areas of MBS broadcast services in an NTN cell | TS 38.331 cl. 6.3.1; TS 38.300 cl. 7.3.1 |

The network broadcasts valid ephemeris information and Common TA parameters for the serving cell, and the UE must have a valid GNSS position, ephemeris and Common TA before connecting to an NTN cell (TS 38.300 clause 16.14.2.2). A UE in RRC_IDLE or RRC_INACTIVE that is accessing NR via NTN access must hold a valid version of SIB19, alongside the MIB and SIB1 to SIB4 (TS 38.331 clause 5.2.2.1).

### SIB27

SIB27 holds `intendedServiceAreaList-r19`, a list of intended service areas, each associated with one or more MBS broadcast services provided in the NTN cell. Each `IntendedServiceAreaInfo-r19` entry has an identifier, `intendedServiceAreaId-r19`, and its area, `areaCoordinates-r19`, given as one of (TS 38.331 clause 6.3.1):

- `polygonArea-r19`: the parameter type Polygon defined in TS 37.355;
- `circleArea-r19`: a centre, `center-r19`, and a radius, `radius-r19`, where each step represents 50 m.

A cell can signal up to 32 MBS broadcast intended service areas (TS 38.331 clause 6.4, `maxNrofMBS-Area-r19`). TS 38.331 sets no UE requirement on the contents of SIB27 beyond those in the procedures and field descriptions that use it (clause 5.2.2.4.29). SIB27 can also be delivered in RRCReconfiguration, in `dedicatedSystemInformationDelivery`, to a UE whose active BWP has no common search space configured (TS 38.331 clause 6.2.2).

### The session-to-ISA link in the MCCH

In the MBSBroadcastConfiguration message on the MCCH, each session's `mbs-SessionAreaList-r19` lists the intended service areas associated with that MBS broadcast session in the NTN cell. If the field is absent, the session can be received within the MBS Service Area (TS 38.331 clause 6.3.6). TS 38.331 clause 5.9.1.1 names SIB27 among the system information used for MBS broadcast, for the ISAs of MBS broadcast services for NTN.

## RRC states

The RRC states do not change over NTN: a UE can receive a broadcast communication service in RRC_IDLE, RRC_INACTIVE and RRC_CONNECTED (TS 38.300 clause 16.10.1). The broadcast MRB configuration procedure applies to UEs in those states and, for an MBS ISA capable UE, while it is located within the ISA associated with the MBS service, if any (TS 38.331 clause 5.9.3.1).

## UE behaviour

- **Starting and stopping reception.** An NTN-capable UE supporting MBS broadcast may initiate the broadcast MRB establishment procedure when it is inside an ISA, and the broadcast MRB release procedure when it leaves the ISA (TS 38.300 clause 16.14.12). In TS 38.331, entering the ISAs associated with an MBS broadcast service the UE is interested in is one of the occasions for broadcast MRB establishment, and leaving them one of the occasions for release (clause 5.9.3.2).
- **Frequency priority in cell reselection.** An MBS ISA capable UE camping on an NTN cell where ISAs are provided, inside the ISA of a service it receives or wants and able to receive it only on a given frequency, may consider that frequency the highest priority during the MBS broadcast session. Outside the ISA, it may consider candidate frequencies on which it cannot receive the service to be of the lowest priority (TS 38.304 clause 5.2.4.1).
- **ISA in SIB27 or target service area in the USD.** The UE may use either. Using them to avoid acquiring the MCCH outside the MBS service area, and to decide on broadcast MRB configuration in NTN, is left to the UE implementation (TS 38.331 clauses 5.9.2.2 and 5.9.3.1, NOTE 2; TS 38.304 clause 5.2.4.1, NOTE 0j).
- **Being inside an ISA.** How the UE determines whether it is in the ISA of an MBS broadcast service is up to the UE implementation (TS 38.300 clause 16.14.12, NOTE). The NG-RAN delivers the NR NTN Intended Service Area to the UE, and the UE handles it as specified in TS 38.331 (TS 23.247 clause 7.3.1, step 9).
