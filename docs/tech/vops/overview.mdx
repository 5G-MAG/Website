<h1>Video Operation Points</h1>
</div>
</div>

:::tip[At a glance]

- **What it is:** the video capabilities and operation points that 3GPP and third-party services can refer to, plus a conformance validator.
- **Why it matters:** video capabilities are largely independent of the service, so one definition supports interoperability across services.
- **Where it stands:** some AVC and HEVC definitions, an encoder API mapping and the validator report format are still for further study.

:::

## Why the specification exists

TS 26.265 V19.3.0, clause 4.1: "Video capabilities are predominantly independent of the service in use." The same clause says the specification defines video capabilities and operation points "such that 3GPP service specifications as well as third-party service providers can refer to the interoperability points defined in this specification." TS 26.265 is new in Release 19: the VOPS work item consolidates video operation points and capabilities previously scattered across several SA4 specifications, and adds the MV-HEVC stereo operation points (TR 21.919 clause 11.5). Earlier releases defined operation points in other specifications, for example HDR in TS 26.116 (Release 15, TR 21.915 clause 12.4.2) and an 8K TV operation point in TS 26.116 (Release 17, TR 21.917 clause 15.2); see the [release history](/standards/vops/evolution).

## What an operation point is

TS 26.265 V19.3.0, clause 3.1: "Operation Point: A collection of discrete combinations of different video representation formats, including spatial and temporal resolutions, colour mapping, transfer functions, and the encoding format."

TS 26.265 V19.3.0, clause 3.1: "Receiver: A device capable of decoding and rendering any bitstream that is conforming to a certain Operation Point."

TS 26.265 V19.3.0, clause 4.2: "Receivers conforming to an Operation Point are able to decode the bitstream and render the included video signal together with the provided metadata."

## Reference architecture

Clause 4.2 (figure 4.2-1) describes a video encoder that turns a video signal into a bitstream, and a decoder that passes the decoded signal and its metadata to rendering (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 4.2). Both can be configured through APIs, which are not normative: "These APIs are not normatively specified but serve as an example reference to configure encoders and decoders as documented in Annex A."

Figure 4.2-2 extends this to a multi-layer bitstream, where "a receiver conforming to Operation Point 1 is able to extract the relevant data and access units of the entire video bitstream to decode video signal 1." (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 4.2)

## Capabilities and operation points

Clause 4.3 defines a video decoding capability and a video encoding capability. Clause 5.1, NOTE: "These clause does not specify whether these capabilities are required, recommended or suggested to be supported." Service specifications decide that (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 4.3; TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 5.1, NOTE).

Clause 6.1, table 6.1-1 gives seven operation points, each with a video format and a decoding capability: (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1))

| Operation point | Video format |
| --- | --- |
| 3GPP-AVC-HD | 3GPP-HD (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1)) |
| 3GPP-HEVC-HD | 3GPP-HD (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1)) |
| 3GPP-HEVC-HD-HDR | 3GPP-HDR (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1)) |
| 3GPP-HEVC-UHD-HDR | 3GPP-HDR (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1)) |
| 3GPP-HEVC-Stereo | 3GPP-Stereo (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1)) |
| 3GPP-MV-HEVC-Main-Stereo | 3GPP-Stereo (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1)) |
| 3GPP-MV-HEVC-Ext-Stereo | 3GPP-Stereo (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 6.1 (table 6.1-1)) |

## What is still open

- Clauses 7.2.2 and 7.2.3: "The definitions for AVC are for further study." and "The definitions for HEVC are for further study." (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 7.2.2; TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause 7.2.3)
- Annex A.2.3, mapping to an encoder API: "This work is for further study." (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause A.2.3)
- Annex B.2.3, the validator's report: "The detailed semantics and the format of the report is for further study." (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause B.2.3)

## Conformance and the validator

Annex B.1 lists the conformance functions: annotated video test signals, reference encoders and configurations, a conformance validator, conforming decoders and conformance bitstreams (TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause B.1; TS 26.265 V19.3.0 clause B.3). Annex B.3: "the conformance validator may be used to test bitstreams claiming to be conformant to an operation point indeed are conforming, or if not, which test assertions are not fulfilled."

The [VOPS Conformance Validator](/validation/vops) is 5G-MAG's validator for these operation points; its status and the specification version it follows are on its [Implementation status](/validation/vops/implementation) page. TS 26.265 V19.3.0 changes the dual-layer HEVC capability (clause 5.3.3) compared with V19.2.0.
